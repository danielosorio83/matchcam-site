Record every match with a live scoreboard.

MatchCam records volleyball matches on your iPhone or Android phone with the score burned into the video, marks every rally, and turns them into highlights.

Live scoreboard

Keep score while you record. The scoreboard is part of the video, so every clip shows the score.

Rallies and highlights

Every point is marked on the timeline. Jump to any rally or export a highlight reel.

Remote scorekeeping

Pair a second phone or an Apple Watch and keep score from the bench.

Tournaments

Organize matches by tournament and round and keep the whole history.

YouTube upload (Pro)

Upload a match to your own YouTube channel with the title, description and set chapters filled in, and keep a tournament's matches in one playlist.
